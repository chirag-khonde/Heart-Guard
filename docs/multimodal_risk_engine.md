# HeartGuard Multimodal Risk Integration Engine (Phase 7)

## 1. Overview & Architecture

The HeartGuard Multimodal Risk Engine unifies objective clinical machine learning predictions with qualitative lifestyle text analysis into a single, comprehensive cardiovascular risk assessment.

Unlike traditional single-modality scoring systems, HeartGuard evaluates both internal physiological indicators (e.g. resting blood pressure, cholesterol, ECG abnormalities) and external behavioral factors (e.g. tobacco use, sedentary habits, sleep deprivation, family history).

## 2. Multimodal 70/30 Formula

The overall risk score is calculated as a weighted linear combination:

$$\text{Overall Risk} = (\text{Clinical Risk} \times 0.70) + (\text{Lifestyle Risk} \times 0.30)$$

Where:
- **Clinical Risk:** Positive-class probability of heart disease from the clinical ML model, expressed as a percentage ($0.0 - 100.0\%$).
- **Lifestyle Risk:** Quantified lifestyle cardiovascular risk score generated by the NLP analyzer ($0 - 100$).
- **Weights:** $70\%$ clinical weight, $30\%$ lifestyle weight ($\sum w = 1.0$).
- **Overall Risk:** Resulting cardiovascular risk score bounded within $[0.0, 100.0\%]$.

## 3. Data Channels & Subsystems

### Clinical ML Channel (70%)
- **Data Source:** Structured physiological parameters adhering to the canonical `HEARTGUARD_FEATURES` schema (Age, Sex, Chest Pain Type, Resting Blood Pressure, Cholesterol, Fasting Blood Sugar, Resting ECG, Maximum Heart Rate, Exercise Angina, ST Depression, Number of Major Vessels).
- **Model Resolution:** Dynamically resolves the best-performing model from `reports/best_model.json` (Logistic Regression, XGBoost, Random Forest, or Multi-Layer Perceptron).
- **Inference:** Preprocesses raw features through the Phase 3 fitted preprocessor, extracts class 1 probability via `model.predict_proba()`, and scales to a $0 - 100\%$ risk metric.
- **Explainability:** Seamlessly attaches SHAP waterfall plots, base values, and clinical driver rankings via `SHAPExplainerService`.

### Lifestyle NLP Channel (30%)
- **Data Source:** Free-text self-reported narrative describing daily habits, diet, smoking, sleep, physical activity, and family cardiac history.
- **Inference:** Evaluates text through `LifestyleAnalyzer` using NLTK tokenization, phrase matching, negation detection, and numeric sleep hour extraction.
- **Scoring:** Aggregates points across 6 predefined domains (Smoking: 25, Family History: 20, Diet: 18, Inactivity: 15, Sleep: 12, Alcohol: 10), capped at $100$.

---

## 4. Operational Thresholds & Action Mapping

HeartGuard implements strict, auditable decision boundaries for triage and guidance:

| Overall Risk Range | Category | Decision Classification | Recommended Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| **$\text{Risk} > 85\%$** | `CRITICAL` | Immediate Attention Required | *"Immediate medical consultation recommended."* |
| **$60\% \le \text{Risk} \le 85\%$** | `APPOINTMENT_RECOMMENDED` | Elevated Cardiovascular Risk | *"Medical appointment recommendation."* |
| **$\text{Risk} < 60\%$** | `MONITORING` | Low / Baseline Risk | *"Regular monitoring recommended."* |

### Boundary Behavior
- **Exact 60.0%:** Belongs to `APPOINTMENT_RECOMMENDED` (60–85% range).
- **Exact 85.0%:** Belongs to `APPOINTMENT_RECOMMENDED` (the specification strictly requires $>85\%$ for Critical).
- **85.01%:** Classifies as `CRITICAL`.

## 6. Safety & Clinical Disclaimers

1. **Non-Diagnostic:** HeartGuard is an academic research prototype. The overall risk score represents an experimental algorithmic estimate and does not constitute a clinical diagnosis.
2. **Alert Preparedness:** The engine calculates `alert_level` for consumption by Phase 8 (Twilio SMS / Emergency Notifications), but does not send external communications or book appointments automatically.
3. **Reproducibility:** All calculations are strictly deterministic given identical clinical metrics and lifestyle narratives.
