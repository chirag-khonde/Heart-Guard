# HeartGuard — Demo Video Script

**Duration:** 8-10 minutes  
**Version:** 1.0.0

---

## Introduction (0:00 - 0:30)

**Visual:** Title slide with HeartGuard logo

**Script:**
"Hello, I'm [Your Name], and this is HeartGuard — an AI-powered system for early heart disease risk prediction with explainable AI. Heart disease is the leading cause of death globally, and early detection can save lives. HeartGuard uses machine learning to predict cardiovascular risk while providing transparent explanations for every prediction."

---

## Problem Statement (0:30 - 1:00)

**Visual:** Problem slide with statistics

**Script:**
"Heart disease affects millions worldwide. Traditional screening relies on expert interpretation of clinical tests, which can be time-consuming and inconsistent. AI can assist, but black-box models are insufficient for clinical decision support. Doctors and patients need to understand WHY a prediction was made. That's where HeartGuard comes in."

---

## Login & Authentication (1:00 - 1:30)

**Visual:** Login page

**Script:**
"HeartGuard starts with secure authentication. Users log in with their email and password. Passwords are hashed with Bcrypt — never stored in plain text. The system supports three roles: Patients, Reviewers, and Administrators. Each role has appropriate access controls."

**Action:** Log in as admin

---

## Patient Dashboard (1:30 - 2:00)

**Visual:** Patient dashboard

**Script:**
"The dashboard shows system status and provides quick access to all features. Patients can see their assessment history, risk trends, and recommendations. The interface is responsive and accessible."

---

## Risk Assessment (2:00 - 3:30)

**Visual:** Risk assessment form

**Script:**
"Let's create a risk assessment. Patients enter clinical data — age, blood pressure, cholesterol, heart rate — and lifestyle information as free text. The NLP analyzer processes this text to extract cardiovascular risk factors."

**Action:** Fill in clinical data and lifestyle text

**Visual:** Prediction results

**Script:**
"The system combines clinical and lifestyle data using a weighted ensemble of four models: Random Forest, XGBoost, Neural Network, and Logistic Regression. The final risk score is 0-100%, with risk categories from Low to Very High."

---

## Explainable AI (3:30 - 4:30)

**Visual:** SHAP explanation page

**Script:**
"Now here's what makes HeartGuard special — explainability. Every prediction comes with a SHAP explanation. SHAP values show exactly how much each feature contributed to the prediction. For example, ST depression increased risk by 15 points, while max heart rate decreased it by 8 points. This transparency builds clinical trust."

**Action:** Show waterfall plot and feature importance

---

## Recommendations (4:30 - 5:00)

**Visual:** Recommendations page

**Script:**
"Based on the risk assessment, HeartGuard generates personalized recommendations — clinical follow-ups, lifestyle modifications, and monitoring suggestions. These are generated by a rule-based engine that considers the patient's specific risk factors."

---

## Doctor Review (5:00 - 5:30)

**Visual:** Review portal

**Script:**
"Clinical reviewers can access submitted assessments, review AI predictions, and record their professional observations. The AI risk scores are read-only — this is an observation tool, not a diagnostic system. This ensures appropriate clinical oversight."

---

## Admin Analytics (5:30 - 6:30)

**Visual:** Admin dashboard, analytics, monitoring

**Script:**
"Administrators have access to aggregated analytics — risk distributions, prediction trends, model performance metrics. They can monitor data drift, data quality, and system health. All analytics show only aggregated data — no patient-identifiable information."

---

## Security (6:30 - 7:00)

**Visual:** Security status page

**Script:**
"Security is built in from the ground up. Role-based access control, IDOR protection, audit logging, input validation, and privacy filtering. Every security-sensitive action is logged. Patients can only see their own data."

---

## Architecture (7:00 - 7:30)

**Visual:** Architecture diagram

**Script:**
"HeartGuard uses a clean architecture: Streamlit frontend, Python backend, four ML models, SHAP explainability, NLP lifestyle analyzer, and six SQLite databases for separation of concerns."

---

## Model Evaluation (7:30 - 8:00)

**Visual:** Model performance charts

**Script:**
"We trained and evaluated four models on the Cleveland Heart Disease dataset — 303 samples, 19 features. The best performer is XGBoost with 0.608 ROC-AUC. These are modest results on a small dataset — HeartGuard is an academic prototype, not a clinical-grade system."

---

## Conclusion (8:00 - 8:30)

**Visual:** Conclusion slide

**Script:**
"HeartGuard demonstrates how AI can assist in heart disease risk prediction while maintaining transparency through SHAP explanations. It enforces security and privacy, supports clinical oversight through the reviewer portal, and provides monitoring for administrators. Thank you for watching."
