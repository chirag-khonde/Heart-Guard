# HeartGuard Reviewer Guide

## What a Reviewer Does

A HeartGuard reviewer is an authorized professional who can view AI-generated cardiovascular risk assessments and record professional observations about them.

**Important Disclaimer:** The review portal is an observation tool, NOT a diagnostic system. AI-generated risk scores, SHAP values, and recommendations are displayed read-only and can NEVER be modified through this portal. Professional review notes are observations only — they are NOT clinical diagnoses, prescriptions, or treatment plans. Do NOT enter diagnostic conclusions, medication recommendations, or treatment decisions into the notes field. Any clinical decision must be made by a qualified healthcare professional acting under their own professional judgment and applicable regulations.

### What You Can Do
- Browse all patient assessments in the system
- View AI-generated risk scores, SHAP explanations, and lifestyle factors (read-only)
- Create or update a professional review record with observations
- Set review status, follow-up flags, and urgency flags
- View your own review history

### What You Cannot Do
- Modify AI-generated risk scores or SHAP values
- Delete or alter assessment data
- Access raw patient lifestyle narrative text
- Perform administrative actions (user management, system configuration)

---

## Logging In as a Reviewer

1. Your admin must create your reviewer account using the `create_reviewer.py` script.
2. You will receive your email and password from the administrator.
3. Open HeartGuard in your web browser.
4. Enter your email and password on the login page.
5. Click **"Log In"**.
6. You will be redirected to the review portal.

Reviewer accounts cannot self-register. Only administrators can create reviewer accounts.

---

## The Review Queue

After logging in, you see the Doctor Review Portal with three tabs:

### KPI Metrics (Top Row)
- **Pending Reviews** — Assessments that have no review record yet (unassigned) plus assessments in PENDING status.
- **In Review** — Assessments currently being reviewed.
- **Reviewed** — Assessments you have completed a review for.
- **Follow-Up Recommended** — Assessments where a follow-up was flagged.
- **Total Assigned** — Total review records assigned to you.

### Review Status Distribution
An expandable chart showing the distribution of your review statuses (PENDING, IN_REVIEW, ACCEPTED, MODIFIED, REJECTED).

## Viewing Assessment Details

### Tab 1: All Assessments
Shows every assessment in the system with columns:
- Assessment ID, Patient ID, Recorded At (UTC), Overall Risk, Risk Category, Review Status, Urgent flag, Follow-up flag.
- Urgency-flagged assessments appear first.
- Select an assessment from the dropdown to view its full details.

### Tab 2: Pending Review
Shows assessments that have NOT yet been assigned a review record. These are the oldest unreviewed assessments first.
- Select an assessment to start a new review.

### Tab 3: My Reviews
Shows review records you have created or last updated.
- Select an assessment to update an existing review.

## Understanding the AI Assessment Panel

When you select an assessment, you see the **AI-Generated Assessment** panel. This is read-only.

### Risk Metrics
- **Overall Risk** — The combined multimodal risk percentage.
- **Clinical Risk** — The clinical ML model risk (70% weight).
- **Lifestyle Risk** — The lifestyle NLP risk (30% weight).
- **Risk Category** — CRITICAL, ELEVATED, or LOWER_RISK.

### Assessment Metadata
- Assessment ID, recorded timestamp (UTC), model version, alert status.

### AI Narrative Summary (Expandable)
A plain-language interpretation of the assessment results generated by the AI system.

### Top Clinical SHAP Factors (Expandable)
- A bar chart showing the top features by SHAP value.
- A table with Feature name, SHAP Value, and Direction (Increases Risk or Decreases Risk).

### Detected Lifestyle Risk Factors (Expandable)
- Table showing Factor name, Severity, and Risk Points for each lifestyle risk factor detected.

**Reminder:** These are AI-generated outputs. They are experimental estimates, not medical diagnoses.

## Understanding SHAP Explanations

SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) values show how the AI model weighted each clinical feature when making its prediction.

### How to Interpret
- **Positive SHAP value** (+) — The feature pushed the prediction **higher** (toward higher risk).
- **Negative SHAP value** (-) — The feature pushed the prediction **lower** (toward lower risk).
- **Larger absolute value** — The feature had more influence on the prediction.

### What SHAP Is NOT
- SHAP values describe **model behavior**, not medical causation.
- A feature with a high SHAP value does not mean it caused heart disease.
- SHAP explains why the model made a particular prediction, not whether the prediction is medically correct.

### Using SHAP in Review
SHAP factors help you understand which inputs the model found most significant for a specific patient. This context can inform your professional observation, but should not be treated as a clinical finding.

## Understanding Lifestyle Factors

The lifestyle analysis section shows risk factors detected from the patient's self-reported lifestyle narrative using a rule-based NLP lexicon.

### Detected Factors Table
Each factor includes:
- **Factor** — Name of the risk factor (e.g., Smoking, Physical Inactivity)
- **Severity** — Low, Moderate, or High
- **Risk Points** — Points contributed to the lifestyle risk score

### Important Context
- The analyzer checks for **predefined indicators only** (smoking, inactivity, unhealthy diet, poor sleep, family history, alcohol).
- Absence of detected factors does not mean the patient has no lifestyle risks.
- The patient's raw lifestyle text is never stored or displayed in the review portal.

## Submitting a Review

### For a New Assessment (No Existing Review)

1. Select an assessment from any of the three tabs.
2. In the **Professional Review** section, fill in the form:
   - **Review Status** — Select from: PENDING, IN_REVIEW, ACCEPTED, MODIFIED, REJECTED, REVIEWED.
   - **Follow-up Appointment Recommended** — Toggle on if a follow-up is warranted.
   - **Flag for Urgent Attention** — Toggle on if the assessment warrants urgent attention.
   - **Professional Notes** — Enter your observations (maximum characters apply).
3. Click **"Save Review"**.
4. The review is created and linked to the assessment.

### For an Assessment You Already Reviewed

1. Go to the **"My Reviews"** tab.
2. Select the assessment you want to update.
3. Modify the status, notes, follow-up, or urgency fields.
4. Click **"Update Review"**.

### Ownership Rules
- You can only update reviews **you created**. If another reviewer has already reviewed an assessment, you will see their review in read-only mode.
- The system enforces this at the database level — attempting to update another reviewer's record will fail with a permission error.

## Review Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **PENDING** | Review record created but not yet completed. Default when first opening an assessment. |
| **IN_REVIEW** | You are actively reviewing this assessment. |
| **REVIEWED** | Review completed (standard). |
| **ACCEPTED** | Review completed — you accept the AI assessment as reasonable. |
| **MODIFIED** | Review completed — you have noted observations that differ from or add context to the AI assessment. |
| **REJECTED** | Review completed — you have concerns about the AI assessment accuracy or applicability. |

## Audit Trail

Every action you take in the review portal is logged in the immutable audit trail:

- **review_created** — When you create a new review for an assessment.
- **review_updated** — When you update an existing review.

Each log entry includes:
- Timestamp (UTC)
- Event type
- Your user ID and role
- Status (SUCCESS or FAILURE)
- Assessment ID and review status

This audit trail is tamper-evident and visible to administrators in the Security & Audit Center. It ensures accountability and traceability for all professional review actions.

### What Is Logged
- Review creation and update events
- Assessment access (via admin dashboard access logs)
- Login and authentication events

### What Is NOT Logged
- The content of your professional notes (only the fact that notes were saved)
- Patient clinical data or lifestyle narratives
- Your password or session tokens
